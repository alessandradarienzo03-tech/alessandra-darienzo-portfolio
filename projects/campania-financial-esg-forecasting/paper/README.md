# Research Paper

This folder contains the research paper accompanying the project:

**Predictive and Explainable Forecasting of Financial and ESG Performance in Private Companies: Evidence from Campania (2015–2024)**

**Authors:** Cristina Arino, Alessandra D'Arienzo, Lucio Di Gioia  
**Institution:** University of Naples Federico II

The paper documents the complete methodology, experimental design and results of the project, including:

- data-quality assessment and preprocessing;
- financial and ESG forecasting;
- benchmarking of Linear Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, Prophet and LSTM;
- model evaluation across company size, geography and macro-sector;
- GenAI-assisted recovery of missing geographic information;
- economic–sustainability positioning for decision support.
